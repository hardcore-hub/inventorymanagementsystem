Overview
The TESDA Region VII Supply and Inventory Management System is a web-based platform designed to streamline, centralize, and improve the management of supplies, inventory records, stock transactions, and procurement activities within TESDA Region VII.

Developed using Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, React 18, and Bootstrap 5, the system provides a user-friendly dashboard for monitoring inventory balances, recording receipts and issuances, managing physical inventory counts, and generating consolidated reports.

The system integrates the following key modules: Inventory Items, Stock Card, Report of Supplies and Materials Issued (RSMI), Report on the Physical Count of Inventories (RPCI), Project Procurement Management Plan (PPMP), and Reports Center.

It incorporates automated reference numbering, stock balance calculations, inventory valuation, low-stock alerts, role-based access controls, and activity logging to support efficient inventory operations.
Purpose
The system aims to:
- Improve the accuracy and accessibility of inventory records.
- Reduce manual encoding, repetitive calculations, and paperwork.
- Strengthen accountability and transparency in supply management.
- Support timely procurement planning and inventory reconciliation.
- Provide reliable information for monitoring, reporting, and management decision-making.
Expected Benefits
By centralizing inventory-related processes, the system helps TESDA Region VII personnel monitor stock movement, identify supply shortages, maintain organized records, and prepare reports more efficiently.

Ultimately, the system supports a more efficient, transparent, accountable, and data-driven approach to supply and inventory management within TESDA Region VII.
